This is a virtual reality development project I created for the NASA HUNCH program. The project is a VR simulation of a lunar habitat, designed to showcase the potential for sustainable living on the moon. The project was developed using Unity and C#, and was designed to be experienced using a VR headset.

The photos were taken from a now lost walkthough video of the habitat, in which each area of an early prototype was explored.

The habitat's design was based in real research, and was made to NASA's specifications as closely as we could, including a full hand-made recreation of the ECLSS Life Support System currently stationed on the ISS, featured on slide 2.

The project also featured a model of a bamboo greenhouse created by a sister team in our program, who's entire project revolved around the research and development of sustainable agriculture in space.

The project competed through several rounds of evaluation, and was one of two VR Habitat projects selected to be presented in front of high-ranking NASA personnel at the Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas. This project was the only functioning VR habitat simulation showcased at the event.
